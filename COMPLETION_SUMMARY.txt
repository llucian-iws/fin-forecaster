📋 DETAILED FEATURES

Data Processing:
  • Fetches 700 days of hourly BTC-USD data
  • Handles yfinance 730-day API limitation dynamically
  • Computes 10 engineered features:
    - log_ret (returns)
    - vol_12h, vol_24h (volatility)
    - RSI (momentum)
    - MACD (trend)
    - BollPct (bands)
    - ATR (volatility)
    - EMA 7/21/50/200 (trends)
    - ema_stack (EMA count)
    - vol_norm (volume)

Regime Detection:
  • 3-state Gaussian HMM
  • States learned from returns + volatility
  • Automatic labeling: BEAR (negative), CHOP (neutral), BULL (positive)
  • Transition probability matrix included

Ensemble Model:
  • Gradient Boosting with 100 decision trees
  • Learning rate: 0.05 (slower, more stable)
  • Max depth: 5 (moderate complexity)
  • Predicts average 24h log-return
  • Training: 80% | Validation: 10% | Test: 10%

Uncertainty Quantification (Triple Layer):
  1. Model Uncertainty: Bootstrap resampling of training data
  2. Conformal Bounds: 90% guaranteed coverage on validation
  3. Scenario Range: 10th-90th percentile across paths

Scenario Analysis:
  • BULL scenario (35% probability): +4% shock (ETF approval)
  • BASE scenario (40% probability): 0% shock (no catalyst)
  • BEAR scenario (25% probability): -6% shock (regulation)
  • 10,000 simulated price paths per scenario
  • Probability-weighted composite forecast

Output Report Includes:
  • Current BTC price & regime
  • Sunday 12 AM UTC price forecast
  • Mean, median, P10, P25, P75, P90 estimates
  • 90% confidence interval
  • MC uncertainty bounds
  • Scenario probabilities
  • P(+5%), P(+10%), P(-5%) metrics
  • 4-panel visualization with:
    - Price + forecast + bands
    - Training loss curves
    - Scenario fan chart
    - Price distribution
